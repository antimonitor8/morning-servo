The reader works on the research team of a robotics training-data company in India. The company collects egocentric (first-person, head- or chest-mounted stereo camera) video of people doing real work in commercial and industrial settings, through local vendors, to sell as training data for robot foundation models.

They want to:
- stay current on AI and robotics: research breakthroughs, new models, deployments, and venture funding
- see many concrete use cases of AI and robotics across industries, especially physical work
- spot where demand for robot training data is heading: which tasks, sectors and data types buyers will need next

They care most about: embodied AI and robot learning, humanoids and manipulation, real deployments with numbers, training data and data collection (egocentric video, teleoperation, simulation, datasets), funding rounds and who is buying, and India's AI and robotics ecosystem and manufacturing.
They care less about: consumer chatbot feature updates, AI celebrity drama, and generic opinion pieces.
